About SFA Globex

SFA Globex FZCO was established in Dubai, UAE, in 2017 for the purpose of trading ferro alloys such as ferro manganese, silico manganese, and ferro chrome, alongside other raw materials required by the steel industry.

The company was founded by Mr. Naresh Rawat and Mr. Rakesh Rawat, veterans of the ferro alloys industry, together with Ms. Gunjan Khandelwal, a young entrepreneur known for her decisive approach. Together, the team brings experience, energy, and practical knowledge to every customer relationship.

SFA Globex began with ferro alloys and expanded its offering to manganese ore, chrome ore, stainless scrap, aluminium scrap, mild steel, stainless steel, ferro molybdenum, and related industrial materials.
